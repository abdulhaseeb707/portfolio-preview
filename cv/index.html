Research

Inflation and Corporate Profitability in Pakistan, FY2006–FY2025

2025 – 2026

Independent research project (working paper, not peer reviewed)

github.com/abdulhaseeb707/inflation-corporate-profitability-pakistan

  • Built a reproducible Python pipeline that harmonises three State Bank of Pakistan financial-statement releases into a 6,683 firm-year panel of 441 PSX non-financial companies; computed ROA matches SBP's published ratios for 99.75% of firm-years from FY2015
  • Estimated fixed-effects, threshold and heterogeneity models with Driscoll–Kraay standard errors and a wild cluster bootstrap; found the inflation–profitability link is weak on average but kinked at about 10% inflation and varies by sector
  • Wrote a 21-page working paper, technical report and executive summary separating nominal from real profitability: real aggregate operating profit was flat over 20 years despite 6.4× nominal growth
