Research

Inflation and Corporate Profitability in Pakistan, FY2006–FY2025

May 2025 – 2026

Independent working paper, not peer reviewed

github.com/abdulhaseeb707/inflation-corporate-profitability-pakistan

  • Began in 2025 as a Stata regression study of inflation and firm-level profitability; rebuilt in Python in 2026 as a twenty-year panel of 6,683 firm-years for 441 listed non-financial companies, from State Bank of Pakistan data.
  • Estimated fixed-effects, threshold and sector models with Driscoll–Kraay errors and a wild cluster bootstrap: the link is weak on average, bends at about 10% inflation and differs by sector.
  • Showed that operating profit grew 6.4 times in rupees but ended about 3% lower after inflation. Wrote a 21-page paper, a two-page summary and slides.
