  4. 04 / 06

    The 10% bend

    Up to about 10% inflation, return on assets rises 0.48 points per point of inflation. Above it, the slope is flat. The bend holds for every threshold from 6% to 15%.

    • Years below 10% inflation
    • Years at or above 10%

What it means

  • Judge performance in inflationary years by real profits, not only by nominal ratios.
  • Exposure to inflation is mostly a sector question.
  • Above roughly 10% inflation, measured profitability stopped improving.

These are associations from observational data, over twenty years with two big inflation episodes. Accounting changes and the use of the CPI rather than producer prices are documented limits.

What I did

  • Harmonised three State Bank of Pakistan Excel releases with different layouts and year conventions into one firm-year panel
  • Matched company accounts to monthly IMF CPI data using each firm's own year-end month
  • Panel econometrics in Python: fixed effects, Driscoll–Kraay standard errors, wild cluster bootstrap, a threshold model and sector-specific slopes
  • Wrote a 21-page working paper, a technical report, an executive summary and an 11-slide deck
  • Kept the whole pipeline reproducible from raw data with one command (uv, Python 3.15)

It started as a Stata project at university in 2025. In 2026 I rebuilt it in Python with twenty years of data.
